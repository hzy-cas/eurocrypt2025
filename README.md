# Overview

Below is a detailed description of the folders:

## 1. `Classical_to_Quantum/`
- **Description**:  The algorithm (Algorithm 2) for converting an AND-depth-s classical circuit into a compact T-depth-s quantum circuit.

  
## 2. `encryption_and_grover_oracle_ProjectQ/`
- **Description**:  The quantum implementations of the Encryption and Grover Oracle for AES-128 based on ProjectQ

  

## 3. `Other_Applications_SKINNY+ASCON/`
- **Description**: The minimal-width and minimal-T-depth implementations (and the codes for generating these implementations) for the S-boxes of SKINNY and ASCON.

  

## 4. `Our_new_circuits_AES+SHA3/`
- **Description**:  Our new quantum circuits for the S-boxes of AES and SHA3. 

  

## 5. `Resource_Estimator/`
- **Description**: Our resource estimation tool.  Two versions: 1) 'T-depth-estimation.py' 'is the Clifford+T version; 2) 'Toffoli-depth-estimation.py' is the NCT version.  

  

## 6. `SAT-based-method-for-minimal_width_NCT/`
- **Description**: Our SAT-based method for constructing minimal-width NCT circuits introduced in Section 5.2. 

  

## 7. `Top_down_approach_Algorithm1/`
- **Description**: The Top-down approach (Algorithm1) using the SAT-based method to find covers.
